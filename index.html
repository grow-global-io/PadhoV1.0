Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Padho offer? +

Padho provides custom websites, native iOS & Android applications, digital distribution tools (for courses, ebooks, test series), secure podcasting, live webinars, merchandise store creation, and modular membership systems built exclusively for online educators.

Do I need technical expertise to use Padho services? +

No, you do not need any coding or technical skills. Padho handles the hosting, deployment, applications compilation, and custom domains configuration while you manage everything from a simple dashboard.

How long does it take to create a website or application with Padho? +

Websites can be launched almost instantly. Custom native mobile applications generally take between 2 to 5 business days for compiling and submitting to the App Store and Google Play Store.

Can I request changes or updates to my website or app after it's live? +

Yes, all course material, products, and site text can be edited directly from your dashboard in real-time. Application updates are pushed automatically through our cloud-sync architecture.

What kind of support is available after the website or app is launched? +

We offer 24/7 dedicated support via WhatsApp, email, and live video assistance to resolve any queries and ensure your students have zero disruption.
